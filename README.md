MediaInfo setup (Raven bS)

1. Copy MediaInfo.java into your Minecraft folder, in keystrokes/scripts.

2. In Raven's Manager, turn on the HTTP setting.

3. Once the script is inside the script folder, Reload scripts and toggle it

4. Right click media-helper.ps1 and open with,
 then select Choose An App On Your PC and find the powershell (default version not 7),
its usually located in "C:\Users\"your user"\AppData\Roaming\Microsoft\Windows\Start Menu\Programs\Windows PowerShell",
you have every right to check what the code has i didnt bother hiding it

5. Play a song in Spotify or a YouTube video in your browser.

6. Configure MediaInfo to your liking. The title and artist show on
   screen. Theme, X, Y and Scale can be changed in its settings.

Optional: auto-start the helper
   Add it as a startup app if you wish to use it without launching the helper every time
